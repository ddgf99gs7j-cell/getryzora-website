Subscriptions

RYZORA Pro is an optional auto-renewing subscription: €4.99 per month or €39.99 per year (prices may vary by country). New, eligible subscribers can try Pro free for 7 days; when the trial ends, the subscription starts automatically unless you cancel at least 24 hours before. Payment is charged to your Apple ID. The subscription renews automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Manage or cancel in Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Purchases and refunds are handled by Apple. RYZORA Free stays free and ad-free; if Pro ends, your alarms keep ringing and your Pro settings stay saved.

How RYZORA works — and its limits

RYZORA helps you get up with alarms, missions and checks on your iPhone. It depends on iOS delivering alarms: keep your iPhone charged and its volume, alarm permission and notifications on — RYZORA shows a warning when it can detect a problem. iOS lets you stop any alarm from the Lock Screen; for alarms with a mission RYZORA then rings again while your morning is still open (up to about 60 minutes after the first ring), but it cannot physically prevent you from switching off or removing it. Do not rely on RYZORA as the only alarm in situations where oversleeping could cause harm.

RYZORA is not a medical device; it does not measure, diagnose or treat sleep. Sleep Mode's fade is an estimate from your phone lying still and locked — never a measurement of sleep. Object recognition can also recognise a clear photo of an object.
