RYZORA – Wake Protocol

Everything RYZORA does.

Only what is in RYZORA 1.0 — no promises about features that are not there yet.

Alarms that work like an alarm

Unlimited alarms, repeat days, snooze and 22 alarm sounds (8 free). RYZORA alarms are scheduled with iOS, so they ring even when the app is closed, in Silent mode or during a Focus.

  • A new alarm starts at the current time
  • Several alarms side by side
  • A wake-sound test so you know your volume

Missions that get you up

Choose what you must do before the alarm stops: a sum (free at every level), find an object with your camera, walk a number of steps or type a phrase (Pro). Wrong answers keep the alarm going; only a completed mission ends it.

  • Several missions in a row (Pro)
  • Emergency options: hold, then type a phrase — always available
  • Your iPhone's emergency call is never blocked

Find an object

Pick the objects you have at home. When the alarm rings RYZORA chooses one at random — fixed for that morning, no re-roll. Recognition runs on your iPhone; no photo is stored or sent. Honest note: like any camera check it can also recognise a clear photo of the object, which is why Wake Checks follow.

  • Objects near your bed never count as leaving the bedroom
  • If the camera fails technically, a step-and-sum fallback takes over

Wake Protocol and Wake Checks

In Wake Protocol and I Must Get Up, RYZORA checks in again a few minutes after your mission. Miss one, or go quiet, and it rings again. I Must Get Up adds a Bedroom Exit: proof that you left the bedroom.

  • Movement Check and Final Check after your mission
  • Each morning ends as secured, recovered or not verified

Guided Sleep and sleep sounds

Three guided sessions — Breathing for Sleep, Deep Relaxation and Body Scan — each with the voice of Clara or Milo (spoken in English), plus seven sleep sounds. Sessions keep playing on the Lock Screen, end on your timer and go quiet before your next RYZORA alarm.

  • Free: Soft rain and Calm shore
  • Pro: all six sessions and all seven sounds
  • Sleep timer 30–120 minutes for sessions, 15–90 for sounds; pause and resume

Wake Insights

See what you actually did each morning: Wake Score with an honest breakdown, mornings secured, Wake Checks on time, time to get up, a calendar, records and streaks. RYZORA never claims to measure your sleep.

  • Free: the last 7 days
  • Pro: 30 days, 90 days and a year

Private by design

No account, no ads, no tracking. Your alarms and mornings stay on your iPhone; you can export or delete everything in Settings.

  • Camera images are analysed on the device and never kept
  • Never your location, microphone or health data
