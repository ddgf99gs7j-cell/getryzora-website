FAQ

My alarm didn't ring

Open RYZORA → Settings and check the alarm status: alarm permission, notifications and the iPhone's “Ringtone and Alerts” volume. Keep your iPhone charged. RYZORA alarms are scheduled with iOS and ring even when the app is closed, in Silent mode or during a Focus.

I stopped the alarm on the Lock Screen — why does it ring again?

iOS lets you stop any alarm from the Lock Screen. If your alarm has a mission, RYZORA rings again while your morning is open — for up to about an hour — until you complete it. Open RYZORA and complete the mission. An alarm without a mission simply stops.

I can't switch my alarm off

During a protected morning an alarm that is already scheduled can't be switched off — on purpose. It ends when the mission is done; a stopped alarm comes back for up to about an hour. Your iPhone's emergency call is never blocked.

My object isn't recognised

Make sure there is light and aim calmly at the whole object. If the camera fails for technical reasons, RYZORA switches to steps and a sum.

Subscriptions and refunds

Manage or cancel in Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Refunds are handled by Apple (reportaproblem.apple.com). “Restore purchases” is on the Pro screen and in Settings → Subscription.

Is Guided Sleep in my language?

The guided sessions are spoken in English; the app itself is in English and Dutch.

Pricing

New, eligible subscribers get 7 days free; then €4.99 a month or €39.99 a year is charged and the subscription renews automatically unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the period ends (iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions).
