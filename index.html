Wake Protocol

Your alarm. Your challenge.

Set an alarm, choose what you must do to stop it, and do it when it rings. Not just a button — a reason to get up.

  • A sum to solve — every level is free
  • Find an object in another room with your camera (Pro)
  • I Must Get Up: the strictest mode, with a Bedroom Exit (Pro)
  • Wake Checks a few minutes later, if you drift back (Pro)
  • Several alarms, repeat days and the sound you like

Object recognition runs on your iPhone. It recognises the object — and, like any camera check, a clear photo of it too. That is why Wake Checks follow your mission.

Guided Sleep

Better nights.
Brighter mornings.

Unwind with guided sessions and calming sleep sounds that end on their own. Your alarm always comes first: a session goes quiet before your next RYZORA alarm.

  • Three guided sessions — Breathing for Sleep, Deep Relaxation, Body Scan — with Clara or Milo (spoken in English)
  • Seven sleep sounds: rain, shore, forest, ambient and piano
  • Keeps playing while your iPhone is locked, then fades out
  • A sleep timer from 30 to 120 minutes

Insights

See your progress.

RYZORA records what you actually did each morning — never a guess about your sleep.

  • Wake Score with an honest breakdown
  • Mornings secured and Wake Checks on time
  • Time to get up, calendar, records and streaks
  • Last 7 days free · 30 days, 90 days and a year with Pro
